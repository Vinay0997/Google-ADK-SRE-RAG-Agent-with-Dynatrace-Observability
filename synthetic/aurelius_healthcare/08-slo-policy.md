# Aurelius service level objectives
patient-portal-api: availability 99.9 percent monthly, p95 latency under 800 ms.
eprescribing-svc: availability 99.95 percent, successful submission rate above 99.5 percent.
lab-results-worker: oldest message age under 5 minutes for 99 percent of the time.
When the monthly error budget is more than 75 percent used, freeze non-critical deploys until the budget recovers.
