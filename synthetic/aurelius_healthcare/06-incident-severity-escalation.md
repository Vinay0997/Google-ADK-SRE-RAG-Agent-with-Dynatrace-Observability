# Incident severity and escalation
Sev1: patient-facing outage or possible PHI exposure. Page primary on-call and incident commander immediately. Update every 15 minutes.
Sev2: major feature degraded, such as e-prescribing or lab results. Page primary on-call. Update every 30 minutes.
Sev3: minor degradation with a workaround. Ticket during business hours.
Primary on-call escalates to secondary after 10 minutes without acknowledgement. A postmortem is required for Sev1 and Sev2 within 5 business days.
