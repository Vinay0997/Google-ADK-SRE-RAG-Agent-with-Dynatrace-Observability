
# Policy Exception Process

**Company:** Aurelius Health Systems  
**Document Type:** RISK_GOVERNANCE  
**Department:** Risk_governance  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Classification:** Internal  

## Purpose
This document defines internal standards, procedures, and expectations for Aurelius Health Systems.
It is intended to support consistent operations, regulatory readiness, and secure handling of data.

## Scope
This document applies to all employees, contractors, and systems operated or managed by Aurelius Health Systems.

## Roles & Responsibilities
- Leadership ensures enforcement and governance.
- Department owners maintain and review this document annually.
- Employees are responsible for compliance.

## Policy / Procedure Details
Aurelius Health Systems follows industry best practices to ensure operational excellence.
Controls, approvals, and documentation are required where applicable.
Deviations must be documented and approved by management.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Review & Maintenance
This document is reviewed at least annually or upon significant organizational or regulatory change.
